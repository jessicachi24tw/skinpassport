# Demo video script (3:00)

**Story in one line:** the same skin, two different trips, two different bags.
**Shape:** a real problem → one traveller → the app solves it step by step → the "aha" (same face, other city) → the payoff (a passport stamp) → why it matters.

Record on the live Vercel site in a phone-size window (or on a real phone with screen recording). Do one **live** YouCam scan with your own selfie in daylight. About 400 spoken words, so speak calmly and leave pauses.

## Before recording
- Prepare a second passport in advance: same selfie, destination **Miami**, for the 1:55 cut.
- Close other tabs and turn off notifications. No background music unless it is royalty-free.
- Third-party trademarks: keep product names and photos on screen briefly and readable only as part of the app. Do not show brand logos full screen, and do not say brand names in the voice-over.

## Script

| **0:00–0:15** Hook | Black screen, then the landing page: the 3D face turning slowly | "Last winter I flew from humid Taipei to freezing New York. Two days in, my skin was tight, red and flaking. The weather changed overnight. My skincare didn't." |

## Storytelling tips
1. **Start with a person, not a feature.** Your own trip in the first 15 seconds makes judges care before they see any UI.
2. **One tension, one release.** The tension is "my skin broke down when the weather changed"; every step after that releases a bit of it.
3. **Say "YouCam" at the moment it works.** Judges need to hear clearly which parts use the API: Skin Analysis at the scan, Skin Simulation at the payoff.
4. **The "aha" is the contrast.** New York vs. Miami on the same face is the clearest proof that the plan really adapts.
5. **End on the business line, then the tagline.** The last thing judges hear should be the name and the promise.
