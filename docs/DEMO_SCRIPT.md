# Demo video script (about 2 minutes 50 seconds)

Record on a phone-size browser window (Chrome DevTools → iPhone 14) at `http://localhost:3000` (the camera needs localhost or HTTPS). Do one **live** YouCam scan; keep "Use demo scan" as a backup.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Title screen | "Travel wrecks skin: dry cabins, winter cold, a UV-11 beach. SkinPassport tells you what your skin needs *before* you go." |
| 0:10–0:35 | **Step 1**: From = **Use my location**; To = "ja" → Japan → "sap" → Sapporo; plane flies; pick dates, **Plane**, tags **Skiing** + **Hiking** → **Continue** | "I start with the trip. Within 16 days we use the live forecast; further out, typical weather for those dates." |
| 0:35–1:05 | **Step 2**: Start face scan → mesh locks on → auto-capture → 3D mesh while YouCam analyzes → report with grey zones and **Trip risk** | "Like a passport check: a 3D face map, then YouCam scores my skin. Moisture is **Weak**, and for this trip its risk is **High**." |
| 1:05–1:25 | **Step 3**: search "cerave" → add AM Lotion SPF 30; add "retinol serum" | "I add what's on my shelf. SkinPassport checks it against the trip." |
| 1:25–1:50 | **Plan**: bullet summary → warnings ("SPF 30 is too low", "pause retinol") → product cards with ingredient reasons | "Every pick is explained by its ingredients: my moisture is Weak, and this cream has ceramides that rebuild the barrier." |
| 1:50–2:10 | Tap **New York, dry winter** vs **Miami, high UV** demo destinations (Step 1 → Edit) | "Same skin, different city, a different plan." |
| 2:10–2:30 | Switch **Value → Premium**, **+ Bag** three items, tick **I already have this** on lip repair → **Create my action plan** | "I choose my budget and what to buy." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Action plan → **Save as image** | "Before the trip, during the trip and a shopping list — adapted to the 7 days I have left. Saved straight to my phone." |
| 2:45–2:50 | Architecture diagram | "Next.js, YouCam Skin AI, live weather, a transparent engine, a L'Oréal Groupe catalog. That's contextual commerce." |

Tips: keep it under 3 minutes and show the YouCam scan clearly.
