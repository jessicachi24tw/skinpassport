# Demo video script (about 2 minutes 30 seconds)

Record on a phone-size browser window (Chrome DevTools → iPhone 14). Do one **live** YouCam scan, then switch to `MOCK_YOUCAM=1`-style demo data only if the live scan fails.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title screen | "Travel wrecks skin: dry cabins, winter cold, a UV-11 beach. SkinPassport tells you what your skin needs *before* you go." |
| 0:15–0:45 | Tap **Upload selfie**, wait for scores | "YouCam AI Skin Analysis scores moisture, oil, redness and dark spots. My moisture is low — 32 out of 100." |
| 0:45–1:05 | Type "Ski trip to Hokkaido next week for 4 days" → **Fill in** | "I just describe my trip. The form fills itself, and we pull the forecast for those exact dates." |
| 1:05–1:35 | Tap **New York, dry winter** | "For dry New York winter, the must-pack items are a ceramide barrier cream, hydrating serum and lip repair. SPF 30 is enough. Each item has a necessity score and the reasons behind it." |
| 1:35–2:00 | Tap **Miami, high UV** | "Same skin, Miami in July: sunscreen jumps to 100 and must be SPF 50+ and water-resistant. The rich cream is dropped for a light gel. The cart changes automatically." |
| 2:00–2:15 | Scroll to pre-trip plan, toggle Essential / Complete kit | "I also get a plan for this week — hydrate now, pause retinol — plus a basic or complete kit." |
| 2:15–2:30 | Architecture diagram | "Next.js front and back end, YouCam for skin, live weather, a transparent scoring engine, and Claude to explain it. That's contextual commerce." |

Tips: keep it under 3 minutes, show the YouCam scan clearly, and end on the cart difference — that's the moment judges remember.
