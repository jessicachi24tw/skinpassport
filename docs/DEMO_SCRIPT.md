# Demo video script (about 2 minutes 50 seconds)

Record on a phone-size browser window (Chrome DevTools → iPhone 14) at `http://localhost:3000` (the camera needs localhost or HTTPS). Do one **live** YouCam scan; keep "Use demo scan" as a backup.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title screen | "Travel wrecks skin: dry cabins, winter cold, a UV-11 beach. SkinPassport tells you what your skin needs *before* you go." |
| 0:15–0:50 | **Start face scan** → the mesh locks onto your face → hold still (auto-capture) → **Analyze this photo** → 3D mesh rotates while the checklist runs → tap **Moisture** on the result | "Like a passport check, it maps my face in 3D, then YouCam AI Skin Analysis scores moisture, oil, redness and spots — and shows me *where*. My moisture is low." |
| 0:45–1:10 | From: **Use my location**. To: type "ja" → Japan → "sap" → Sapporo. Watch the plane fly | "Country first, then city — the list narrows as I type. The map shows my route and flight time." |
| 1:10–1:25 | Dates, tap **Plane** + **Train**, tap **Skiing** + **Hiking** tags | "I add how I'm travelling and what I'll do. Tags go straight into my notes, and I can add more later." |
| 1:25–1:50 | Tap **New York, dry winter** | "Same skin, New York in January: much colder and drier than home. Barrier cream, hydrating serum and lip repair become must-packs. SPF 30 is enough." |
| 1:50–2:15 | Tap **Miami, high UV** | "Miami in July: sunscreen jumps to 100 — SPF 50+ and water-resistant. The rich cream is swapped for a light gel. The cart changes automatically." |
| 2:15–2:25 | Switch **Value → Premium**, tick **I already have this** on sunscreen | "I pick my budget, and anything I already own comes off the shopping list — with a reminder of the SPF it needs." |
| 2:25–2:40 | Tap **Details ›** on the after-sun gel → product page → **Add to bag** → open the bag | "Every pick has a product page that explains why it's in *my* plan, how to use it, and alternatives." |
| 2:40–2:50 | Architecture diagram | "Next.js front and back end, YouCam for skin, live weather, a transparent scoring engine, and Claude to explain it. That's contextual commerce." |

Tips: keep it under 3 minutes, show the YouCam scan clearly, and end on the cart difference between New York and Miami.
